FAQ

Questions we hear first

Clear answers before a consult: who we are, who we treat, and how recommendations are made.

Is this a spa?

No. K Beauty Clinic is a K-beauty aesthetics clinic. We do not offer spa menus, physiotherapy, wellness programmes, naturopathic care or IV therapy.

Who is the clinic for?

Women across Greater Vancouver who appreciate Korean aesthetics and culture, typically 35–60+. Care is offered to Korean, Chinese, other Asian and Caucasian clients.

What happens in a consultation?

A professional assessment comes first. We explain suitability, process and recovery in plain language, then build a personalized plan with realistic expectations. There is no pressure selling.

Do you only treat Asian skin?

Korean clinical knowledge of Asian skin is a centre of authority, but treatment is inclusive. We see women of all backgrounds who value Korean aesthetic techniques.

Which treatments do you offer?

Injectables, advanced lasers and light, medical skincare, skin rejuvenation, and signature programmes built around one concern—not a salon menu.

How do you talk about results?

We show realistic outcomes and the path to improvement. We do not make miracle claims or promise a single-session transformation.

Where is the clinic?

Greater Vancouver, beginning with Coquitlam, the Tri-Cities and nearby communities.
