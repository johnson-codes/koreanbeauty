**Brand focus:** Premium Korean medical aesthetics for women across Greater Vancouver  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Logo status:** In development

---

## 1. Brand Core

### Brand Essence

> **Medical confidence with Korean refinement.**

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Category | Premium Korean medical aesthetics |
| Audience | Women across Greater Vancouver who appreciate Korean aesthetics and culture |
| Difference | South Korean cosmetic experience, expertise with Asian skin and extensive treatment history |

### Positioning Statement

Korean Signature Aesthetics is a premium medical-aesthetics clinic serving women across Greater Vancouver who appreciate Korean beauty and culture. Led by extensive South Korean cosmetic experience, the clinic combines advanced treatments, medical credibility and personalized care to deliver confident, visible results.

### Strategic Exclusion

The brand is not positioned around wellness, naturopathic care or IV therapy. Communication must remain focused on injectables, advanced lasers, medical skincare and facial aesthetic treatments.

## 7. Voice and Writing

The voice should be **professional, educational, reassuring and results-focused**.

| Voice Trait | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | Specific language, calm confidence and correct treatment terminology | Casual slang or over-familiarity |
| Educational | Explain what, why, suitability, process and recovery | Information overload or unexplained jargon |
| Reassuring | Realistic expectations, safety and thoughtful guidance | Fear-based selling |
| Results-focused | Evidence, progress and client goals | Guaranteed, perfect or permanent claims |

### Writing Principles

- Lead with the client concern, then explain the professional solution.
- Use plain English first; add clinical terms only when they improve understanding.
- Describe Korean expertise through experience, methods and standards—not stereotypes.
- Write inclusively for Korean, Chinese, other Asian and Caucasian women.
- Always qualify results and avoid medical or cosmetic guarantees.

### Preferred Language

- Personalized plan
- Professional assessment
- Advanced Korean aesthetics
- Visible improvement
- Realistic results
- Confidence

### Words to Avoid

- Flawless
- Perfect
- Miracle
- Instant transformation
- Guaranteed
- Cheap
- Beauty defect

| Excluded focus | Wellness, naturopathic care and IV therapy should not lead the identity or navigation. |

### Naming Formula

> **[Korean or Signature cue] + [clear client benefit] + [treatment type or program]**

**Good examples:**

- Signature Pigmentation Program
- Korean Skin Refinement
- Advanced Facial Contouring
- Korean Glass Skin Program
- Signature Facial Refinement Plan

**Avoid:**

- Korean words without explanation
- Cute treatment names
- Exaggerated transformation claims

## 10. Colour Strategy

All three proposed palettes avoid strong red and blue. Each uses a warm-neutral foundation so photography and real skin tones remain the visual focus.

### Palette Comparison

| Palette | Strategic Character | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Hanji Forest—Recommended** | Strongest balance of Korean refinement, medical trust and warmth | Master brand, website, signage and clinical materials |
| **02 Seoul Stone** | Neutral, modern and architectural | Premium editorial campaigns and minimalist interiors |
| **03 Champagne Porcelain** | Softer, more feminine and beauty-forward | Social campaigns, client materials and premium promotions |

### Colour Usage Principle

- 60% light neutral
- 25% dark anchor
- 10% supporting neutral
- 5% accent

Accent colour should guide attention rather than decorate every element.

## 14. Typography

The typography system should combine premium editorial character with clinical readability and support English and Korean.

| Role | English Recommendation | Korean Recommendation | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display and headlines | DM Serif Display | Noto Serif KR | Major headlines, campaign statements and selected service titles |
| Body and interface | Inter | Noto Sans KR | Website, forms, descriptions, navigation and captions |
| System fallback | Arial or Helvetica | Noto Sans KR | Office documents and platforms without brand fonts |

### Hierarchy

| Level | Style | Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Display serif, 38–52 px | Short, confident and spacious |
| H2 | Display serif or bold sans, 26–34 px | Major sections |
| H3 | Sans serif semibold, 18–22 px | Services and cards |
| Body | Sans serif regular, 16–18 px | Minimum 16 px for website reading |
| Caption | Sans serif medium, 13–14 px | Use sparingly and maintain contrast |

Use serif typography for premium Korean-editorial character and sans serif typography for medical clarity. Never use script fonts for body copy or treatment information.

## 15. Photography and Graphic Language

### Photography Direction

| Area | Direction |
|---|---|
| People | Feature Korean, Chinese, other Asian and Caucasian women; prioritize the 35–60+ audience while maintaining age diversity |
| Skin | Retain natural texture; use light retouching and avoid impossible perfection |
| Clinic | Use real practitioners, real rooms and real equipment whenever possible |
| Before-and-after | Standardize angle, expression, lighting, crop and timing; include consent and results-vary language |
| Mood | Clean, bright and refined with soft directional light |
| Composition | Calm close portraits, uncluttered backgrounds and purposeful negative space |

### Graphic Elements

- Use a restrained signature-line motif to connect sections or highlight treatment journeys.
- Use minimal icons with consistent line weight.
- Use clean diagrams when they improve treatment understanding.
- Avoid cute graphics, generic stock imagery, stereotypes and short-lived trends.

### Before-and-After Priority

Before-and-after evidence is a primary brand asset. Accuracy, consent, consistent conditions and realistic presentation are mandatory.
