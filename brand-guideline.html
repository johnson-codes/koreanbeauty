Positioning Statement

K Beauty Clinic is a K-beauty aesthetics clinic serving women across Greater Vancouver who appreciate Korean beauty and culture. Led by extensive South Korean cosmetic experience, the clinic combines advanced treatments, medical credibility and personalized care to deliver confident, visible results.

Strategic Exclusion

The brand is an aesthetics clinic. It is not a spa, physiotherapy clinic, wellness studio, naturopathic practice or IV lounge. Communication must remain focused on injectables, advanced lasers, medical skincare and facial aesthetic treatments.

07

Voice and Writing

The voice should be professional, educational, reassuring and results-focused.

Voice TraitUseAvoid
ProfessionalSpecific language, calm confidence and correct treatment terminologyCasual slang or over-familiarity
EducationalExplain what, why, suitability, process and recoveryInformation overload or unexplained jargon
ReassuringRealistic expectations, safety and thoughtful guidanceFear-based selling
Results-focusedEvidence, progress and client goalsGuaranteed, perfect or permanent claims

Writing Principles

  • Lead with the client concern, then explain the professional solution.
  • Use plain English first; add clinical terms only when they improve understanding.
  • Describe Korean expertise through experience, methods and standards—not stereotypes.
  • Write inclusively for Korean, Chinese, other Asian and Caucasian women.
  • Always qualify results and avoid medical or cosmetic guarantees.

Preferred Language

  • Personalized plan
  • Professional assessment
  • Advanced Korean aesthetics
  • Visible improvement
  • Realistic results
  • Confidence

Words to Avoid

  • Flawless
  • Perfect
  • Miracle
  • Instant transformation
  • Guaranteed
  • Cheap
  • Beauty defect

Approved Slogans

Public-facing slogans are confined to these three ranked lines. Do not invent additional taglines for ads, signage or the website. They are not medical claims and must not be presented as a guarantee of outcome.

Warm K-Beauty tokens

These five tokens match the live website. Do not introduce a second palette.

Olive Green establishes medical trust without relying on conventional blue. Porcelain and Soft Blush bring warmth next to skin; Burnt Terracotta is the booking colour, not a brand-wide fill.

Brand reasoning

The brand essence is medical confidence with Korean refinement. Olive Green carries clinical responsibility without hospital blue or spa mint. Porcelain is the 60% ground so real skin and before-and-after photography stay dominant. Soft Blush is a supporting skin-adjacent tone, not cute pink. Burnt Terracotta is the 5% CTA: warm enough to book, never used as body text.

  • Personality: Clinical with warmth, premium but welcoming.
  • Values: Safety and professionalism in Olive Green; Korean warmth in Porcelain and Blush, not in costume or flags.
  • Watch-out: Do not push Olive Green toward mint. Do not use Soft Blush as spa pink. Do not use Burnt Terracotta for long copy or large fills.

Graphic Elements

  • Use a restrained signature-line motif to connect sections or highlight treatment journeys.
  • Use bold, large icons (weight 700, 32px) so treatments and CTAs scan quickly. Do not use hairline icons.
  • Use clean diagrams when they improve treatment understanding.
  • Avoid cute graphics, generic stock imagery, stereotypes and short-lived trends.

Before-and-after evidence is a primary brand asset. Accuracy, consent, consistent conditions and realistic presentation are mandatory.
