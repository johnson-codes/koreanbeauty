The clinic

Who we are

K Beauty Clinic is a K-beauty aesthetics clinic. We combine advanced treatments with professional assessment so you feel informed, safe and confident—not sold a package.

We are not a spa, physiotherapy clinic, wellness studio, naturopathic practice or IV lounge. Care stays focused on injectables, advanced lasers, medical skincare and facial aesthetic treatments.

The path

A doctor’s journey, city by city

From Gangnam in 2010 to Coquitlam in 2026. Each chapter is a place and a practice.

  1. 2010–2018

    Seoul, Gangnam

    Medical school, eight years

    Gangnam is where the work began: eight years of medical school and a doctor degree. Clinical responsibility was formed here—assessment first, then treatment.

  2. 2018–2020

    Seoul

    Aesthetic practice in Korea

    Two years of beauty practice in Korea: injectables, skin quality and the standards of Gangnam clinic care. Korean expertise as daily work, not a decorative theme.

  3. 2020–2022

    Tianjin, China

    Practice across cultures

    Two years of practice in Tianjin. Care that moves between Korean and Chinese aesthetic culture, with the same clinical judgment and honest expectations.

  4. 2022–2024

    Toronto

    Doctor degree in Canada

    Two years in Toronto for a Canadian doctor degree. The same medical discipline, now in the language and standards of care in this country.

  5. 2024–2026

    Vancouver

    Practice in Greater Vancouver

    Two years of practice in Vancouver. Women who value Korean aesthetics, seen in person, with plans that fit this city—not a packaged menu.

  6. 2026

    Coquitlam

    K Beauty Clinic opens

    The clinic begins. The path from Gangnam to Coquitlam becomes a place of care: professional assessment, honest guidance and a personalized plan.

Care path

Our clinical process

A structured path from assessment to follow-up, explained in plain language, with honest expectations at every step.

  1. clinical_notes

    Consultation

    We start with a professional assessment of your concern, history and goals. Suitability is explained clearly, with no pressure to book a treatment.

  2. assignment

    Personalized plan

    You receive a recommended path: what, why, recovery and realistic expectations. Combined programmes are only suggested when they serve the concern.

  3. health_and_safety

    Treatment

    The agreed protocol is delivered with clinical care—injectables, laser and light, medical skincare or rejuvenation as planned.

  4. healing

    Aftercare

    You leave with clear aftercare so recovery stays measured. We do not skip instructions or over-promise downtime.

  5. visibility

    Review

    Follow-up checks progress and decides the next step. Results are assessed honestly, including when more time or a different approach is needed.
