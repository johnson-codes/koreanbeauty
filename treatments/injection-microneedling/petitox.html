FAQ

Questions we hear first

Is the treatment painful?

Sensation varies by protocol and the individual. We explain what you are likely to feel before treatment, and we do not describe it as painless if it is not.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the concern. A plan is made after assessment. We do not promise a single-session transformation.

Is it suitable for Asian skin?

Korean clinical knowledge of Asian skin is a centre of authority, and care is inclusive. Suitability is always assessed individually.

What is the downtime?

Recovery is explained before treatment so expectations stay honest. We do not over-promise downtime.

Who should wait?

Some medical history, medications or skin states mean a treatment is not suitable. That decision is made in consultation, before any treatment is offered.
