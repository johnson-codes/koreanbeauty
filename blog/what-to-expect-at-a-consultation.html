What to expect at a consultation

A professional assessment comes first. Here is how a first visit at K Beauty Clinic usually unfolds.

A consultation at K Beauty Clinic in Coquitlam starts with your concern, history and goals—not a packaged spa menu.

We explain suitability, recovery and realistic expectations in plain language before any treatment is offered. Combined plans are only suggested when they serve the concern.

You leave with a clear next step: treatment when suitable, more time when needed, or a different approach when that is more honest.
